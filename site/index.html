What stands out

Food hardship and dental access move together. Living near a grocery does not. CD 9 is closer to markets than CD 8 and still has the worst visit rate.

After poverty, clinician density still lines up with who gets care. California has dentists. Too few take Medi-Cal where need is highest.

Usable dentist capacity

LA has many licensed dentists. Usable Medi-Cal capacity drops fast.

For SCOPE LA

  • Start in CD 9 and CD 8. More Medi-Cal chairs, school and clinic hours that fit working families, help with the ride.
  • Grow dentists who take Medi-Cal. Loan help tied to South LA. Dental therapists and hygienists in safety-net clinics.
  • Food retail helps. It is not enough alone. Keep Good Food Zone work. Pair it with dental access.
  • Measure usable care. Medi-Cal dentists per ZIP, wait times, visit rates. Not license counts citywide.
